    How to play

    Every answer is an attack. Each enemy brings a question, like “Name an animal, in Latin.” Type an answer and press Enter. A correct answer damages the enemy, and the rarer it is, the harder it hits.

    RarityMeaningDamageExample (animals)

    The enemy strikes back on a timer. Wrong answers and repeats hurry it along. Answer fast with common words, or dig for a rare one: that trade-off is the whole game.

    Each enemy has a rule. Harpies feed on common words; the Sphinx wants alphabetical order; Echo ignores anything you said in a past descent; the Gorgon hides the question. Every fifth Gradus a guardian waits: Cerberus, Charon, Minos.

    Between floors, choose a gift of the gods, such as double damage for chaining last letter to first letter, or for very short words. Gifts last for one descent only. Nothing carries over except what you learn.

    The descent never ends. It only gets stranger. Words you lean on across many descents grow worn and hit one tier weaker, so keep learning new ones.

    Schola (class mode) uses only your class handouts. Open questions work as above. Drills ask for one exact answer (an ending, a form, a case); every right answer hits and the next item appears. Tab skips a drill item and shows you its answer.

    Spelling: macrons and capitals don’t matter, j = i and v = u. In open questions, a one-letter slip on a long word still counts, at 80% damage. Drills need exact spelling, since one letter is the whole point of an ending.
